**Title:** Daphnia propagation

**Date:** 27th January 2026

**Aim:** Propagating my *Daphnia* monoculture to have organisms for the experiment.

**Material:**

- Zoo-COMBO medium (1 litre per new jar)

**Steps:**

1.  Bring *Daphnia* monoculture up to the experimental lab 1.

2.  Pour about **150 ml of medium in each jar** (to make it easier to pour, transfer the medium from the large bottle into a smaller Becker).

3.  Transfer the lines. For each line:

    1.  Take the two replicates and a new jar with inside medium.

    2.  Under a light and above a black cloth, use a glass pipette to pipette into the new jar about 7 small Daphnia, 7 medium Daphnia, and 7 large Daphnia from the most recent culture. If the most recent culture is not doing well, transfer from the older culture.

    3.  Put the used glass pipette into a glass jar with written “used”.

    4.  Write with a sharpy today’s date on the newest culture.

    5.  Transfer the label and lid from the older jar to the newest culture (if the lid has fungi growing on it, replace it).

4.  Bring Daphnia back.

    1.  Place the two replicates of each line on two different trays, matching their order, so that next time it’s easy to find them (randomise where each line is on each tray).

    2.  Bring Daphnia back to the room.

5.  Clean

    1.  Feed Jana’s fish. Keep in a large jar about 300 ml of old culture, filter with a funnel and filter the Daphnia from the old cultures, resuspend Daphnia in the 300 ml, and feed them to the fish.

    2.  Plug the medium back into the aquarium pump in the experimental lab 1.

    3.  Rinse jars with demineralised water and scrub the bottom with a scrub brush.

    4.  Place the used jars in the dish washer with dish-wash specific lab soap, press program F.

    5.  Leave glass pipette in demineralised water for 24 hours.

    6.  Rinse the pipes by pulling the pipette holder up and down 10 times.

    7.  Bring glass pipettes to be autoclaved.
