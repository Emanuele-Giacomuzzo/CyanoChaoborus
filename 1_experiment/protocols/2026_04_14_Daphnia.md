**Title:** Create a new cohort of Daphnia jars

**Date:** Tuesday 14th April 2026

**Aim:** Creating jars to start Daphnia cultures, which will be raised on a mix of *Tetradesmus* and *Synechococcus* for 5 days and then provided to Chaoborus.

**Material:**

- Daphnia stock populations

- Zoo-COMBO (7 L)

- Glass pipette and pipette bulb

- Daphnia light table

- Colourful tape

**Steps:**

1.  Create 7 jars with 800 ml of Zoo COMBO, labelled as “EG – Today’s date – Daphnia magna – Mixed diet”.

2.  Pipette 10 individuals from the stock cultures into each newly created jar.

3.  Use some of the Daphnia jars depleted of adults to feed the Chaoborus cultures.
