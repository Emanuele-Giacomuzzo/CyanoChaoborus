**Title:** Create Zoo-COMBO

**Date:** 26th January 2026

**Aim:** Creating a COMBO medium (Kilham et al. 1998) to be used to feed Daphnia. We refer to it as Zoo-COMBO. As we do not want the excessive growth of phytoplankton, as it would clog Daphnia’s respiratory system, we’ll leave out the source of nitrogen (sodium nitrate) and phosphorus (potassium phosphate dibasic) present in the phytoplankton COMBO medium (Phyto-COMBO), also not to have different growths of phytoplankton.

**Use of Zoo-COMBO:** As the Zoo-COMBO needs to air for at least 24 hours before it can be used, it’s better to make it at least two days before using it. The following protocol is to create 10 litres (but normally make 7 litres) of Zoo-COMBO, so if you want to create a different amount of Zoo-COMBO, you need to adjust this protocol. Let the Zoo-COMBO aerate for 24 hours before you can use it. You can leave the Zoo-COMBO on the countertop for up 10-14 days.

**Material:**

- Stock 1 – Calcium chloride dihydrate (CaCl<sub>2</sub> 2H<sub>2</sub>O) 36.76 g/l

- Stock 2 – [Magnesium sulphate heptahydrate](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/DK/en/substance/magnesiumsulfateheptahydrate2464710034998) (MgSO<sub>4</sub> 7H<sub>2</sub>O) 36.97 g/l

- Stock 5 – Sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO<sub>3</sub>) 12.60 g/l

- Stock 6 – Sodium metasilicate nonahydrate (Na<sub>2</sub>SiO<sub>3</sub> 9H<sub>2</sub>O) 28.42 g/l

- Stock 7 – Boric acid (H<sub>3</sub>BO<sub>3</sub>) 24.00 g/l

- Algae trace element (ATE, see Kilham et al. 1998) <sup>\*</sup>

- Animal trace elements (ANIMATE, see Kilham et al. 1998) <sup>\*</sup>

- Vitamin solution (VIM, see Kilham et al. 1998) <sup>\*</sup>

\*If empty notify Vana.

**Steps:**

1.  If the VIM is not in the fridge, get it from the freezer, and thaw it with your hands.

2.  Prepare a solution of water, stocks, trace elements, and vitamins. <u>Every time you use a chemical, rinse the pipette tips with ultra-pure water</u>.

    1.  Fill a Duran bottle with 10 litres of ultra-pure water (MQ) (rinse beforehand)

    2.  Add 10 ml of each stock solution (1-2, 5-7) and trace elements.

    3.  Add 5 ml of VIM.

3.  Set up the bottle to get oxygenated

    1.  Place an aerating tube with an aquarium bubbler in the Duran bottle and plug it into an aquarium pump.

    2.  Cover with aluminium foil.

    3.  Label the bottle.

4.  Put material back into place (stock solutions on their shelves and ANIMATE, ATE, and VIM in the fridge) and bring to wash the Beckers used to rinse the pipette tips.

**References:**

Kilham, S.S., Kreeger, D.A., Lynn, S.G., Goulden, C.E. & Herrera, L. 1998. COMBO: a defined freshwater culture medium for algae and zooplankton. *Hydrobiologia*. 377:147–59.
