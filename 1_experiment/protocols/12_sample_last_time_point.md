# Sample the algae

## 1. Prepare a mix of the two algae

1. Bring up the large algal cultures.
2. Transfer algae into two beakers.
3. Replace the phytoplankton COMBO medium with Zoo-COMBO for each alga:
   1. Add **48 mL** of algae to **8 Falcon tubes** using the autopipette and the pipette tip labelled with the species name.
   2. Centrifuge the Falcon tubes for **10 minutes at 28,000 rpm** to pellet the algae.
   3. While the centrifuge is running, switch on the spectrophotometer.
   4. Carefully discard the phytoplankton COMBO immediately after centrifugation to avoid resuspending the pellet.
   5. Add **48 mL** of Zoo-COMBO to each Falcon tube and combine all tubes into one beaker.
4. Measure the optical density (OD) of the algae.
   - The spectrophotometer requires approximately **15 minutes** to warm up.
   - The cuvette is stored in ethanol.
   - Before use, rinse the cuvette with demineralised water followed by ethanol.
   - Dry the cuvette using lens paper.
5. Measure Zoo-COMBO alone by selecting:

   ```
   Photometric → Enter
   ```

6. Measure the algae by selecting:

   ```
   GOTOWL
   Wavelength: 480 nm
   Start
   ```

7. Mix the algae so that **50% of the carbon originates from *Tetradesmus obliquus*** and **50% from *Synechococcus elongatus***.

---

## 2. Set up the filtration system

1. Assemble the vacuum filtration unit and connect it to the wall gas supply.
2. Place a **GF/C filter** (pre-combusted at **550 °C for 2 hours**) onto the filtration unit using clean forceps.
   - Clean forceps with ethanol.
   - Flame sterilise while holding the forceps horizontally.
3. Place the cylinder weights on top of the filters.
4. Slowly apply suction, ensuring that the pressure remains **below 0.2 bar**.

---

## 3. Filter the samples

For each sample:

1. Mix the culture thoroughly.
2. Pipette the culture onto the filter.
3. Adjust the vacuum if necessary so that no liquid remains on top of the filter.
4. Record the filtered volume.

**Volume filtered:** ____________ mL

5. Transfer the filter to a 24-well plate.
   - Turn off the suction.
   - Remove the cylinder weights.
   - Using clean flame-sterilised forceps, transfer the filter to the well plate.
6. Clean the filtration unit.
   - Replace the cylinder weights.
   - Turn the suction back on.
   - Wash the meshes and cylinders with ethanol followed by demineralised water.
7. Dry the filters overnight in a **50 °C oven**.

# Sample *Daphnia*

1. Collect the *Daphnia* remaining in each *Chaoborus* jar.
   - Filter each replicate separately.
   - Store each sample in a Falcon tube containing **70% ethanol**.
2. Collect **1,000 *Daphnia*** from the cohorts started **five days earlier**.
   - Keep these animals in their original COMBO medium.
3. Separate small and large *Daphnia* using a **1 mm mesh**.
4. Preserve:
   - **20 small *Daphnia*** in ethanol.
   - **20 large *Daphnia*** in ethanol.

These individuals will later be measured to compare body size between the two size classes.

> [!TIP]
> **Folding filters**
>
> To transfer a filter:
> 1. Slide one arm of the forceps underneath the filter.
> 2. Hold the filter gently in place with a second pair of forceps.
> 3. Fold the filter in half.
> 4. Fold it in half again so it forms a quarter circle before placing it in the well plate.
