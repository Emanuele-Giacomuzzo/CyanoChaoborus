**Title:** Phytoplankton COMBO medium

**Date:** Tuesday 10th February 2026

**Aim:** Creating a COMBO medium (Kilham et al. 1998) to be used to grow phytoplankton to then be fed to Daphnia. The following is to create 5 litres of medium, so if you want to create a different amount of medium, you need to adjust this protocol.

**Material:**

Name Concentration

- Stock 1 – Calcium chloride dihydrate (CaCl<sub>2</sub> 2H<sub>2</sub>O) 36.76 g/l

- Stock 2 – [Magnesium sulphate heptahydrate](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/DK/en/substance/magnesiumsulfateheptahydrate2464710034998) (MgSO<sub>4</sub> 7H<sub>2</sub>O) 36.97 g/l

- Stock 3 – Potassium phosphate dibasic (K<sub>2</sub>HPO<sub>4</sub>) 8.71 g/l

- Stock 4 – Sodium nitrate (NaNO<sub>3</sub>) 85.01 g/l

- Stock 5 – Sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO<sub>3</sub>) 12.60 g/l

- Stock 6 – Sodium metasilicate nonahydrate (Na<sub>2</sub>SiO<sub>3</sub> 9H<sub>2</sub>O) 28.42 g/l

- Stock 7 – Boric acid (H<sub>3</sub>BO<sub>3</sub>) 24.00 g/l

- Algae trace element (ATE, see Kilham et al. 1998) <sup>\*</sup>

- Animal trace elements (ANIMATE, see Kilham et al. 1998) <sup>\*</sup>

- Vitamin solution (VIM, see Kilham et al. 1998) <sup>\*</sup>

- TES buffer

\*If empty, notify Vana.

**Steps:**

*Same day*

1.  If the VIM is not in the fridge, thaw it with your hands.

<!-- -->

1.  Prepare a solution of water, stocks, trace elements, and vitamins. Every time you use a chemical, rinse the pipette tips with ultra-pure water.

    1.  Fill a Duran bottle with 5 litres of ultra-pure water (MQ) (rinse beforehand)

    2.  Add 5 ml of each stock solution and trace elements (ATE, ANIMATE). Each time you use a pipette tip, rinse it with distilled water before putting it back.

    3.  Add 2.5 ml of VIM. Rinse pipette tip with distilled water before putting it back.

    4.  Add 0.575 g of TES buffer.

2.  Prepare the bottle. Add tubes, lid, and slightly let the metal part unscrew.

3.  Put material back into place (stock solutions on their shelves and ANIMATE, ATE, and VIM in the fridge) and bring to wash the Beckers used to rinse the pipette tips.

4.  Autoclave overnight.

*Day after*

1.  Get the medium out of the autoclave, screwing the metal part tight.

2.  Label the bottle.

**References:\**

Kilham, S.S., Kreeger, D.A., Lynn, S.G., Goulden, C.E. & Herrera, L. 1998. COMBO: a defined freshwater culture medium for algae and zooplankton. *Hydrobiologia*. 377:147–59.
