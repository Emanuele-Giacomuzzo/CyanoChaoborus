**Title:** Create Chaoborus jars

**Date:** Tuesday 28th April 2026

**Aim:** Create new jars for Chaoborus and feed them with Daphnia.

**Material:**

| *Prepared* | *Amount* | *Material* | *Purpose* |
|----|----|----|----|
| ☐ | 7 | Falcon tubes (50 ml) | To sample and distributing Daphnia and Chaoborus |
| ☐ |  | Eppendorf tubes | To put the Chaoborus inside |
| ☐ | 11 L | Zoo COMBO | To create new Daphnia and Chaoborus jars |
| ☐ |  | Pasteur pipette | To suck water out of samples to be frozen |
| ☐ |  | Becker (3L) | To mix the Chaoborus larvae |

**Steps:**

1.  Prepare the Daphnia.

    1.  *Prepare the falcon tubes for feeding & sampling.*

        1.  Mix the Daphnia babies from the oldest cohort into a 10 L bucket and then split it into two 5 L buckets.

        2.  Pipette 100 babies into each of four Falcon tubes.

        3.  Pipette 20 babies into a Falcon tube to later analyse their body size (pipette out as much COMBO as possible and add 70% ethanol).

2.  Prepare the Chaoborus.

    1.  *Create jars.* Create 4 jars with 800 ml of Zoo COMBO for Chaoborus to be fed with Daphnia.

    2.  *Record Chaoborus.* Record how many Chaoborus died or pupated. If they died, take them away; if they pupated, keep them in a Falcon tube.

    3.  *Pipette Chaoborus into newly created jars.*

    4.  *Sample Daphnias left.* Filter the Daphnia left in each of the replicates and keep them in a Falcon tube with 70% ethanol.

    5.  *Feed with Daphnia.* Add from the falcon tubes created at step 1.
