**Title:** Grow *Nannochloropsis limnetica.*

**Date:** Monday 2nd February 2026

**Aim:** Inoculate *Nannochloropsis limnetica* (SAG 18.99) in Phytoplankton COMBO to grow it enough so that we can calculate an optical density (OD) curve. The climate room we refer to throughout this protocol is room 20-1-145 and is kept at 20 degrees Celsius.

**Material:**

- Phytoplankton COMBO medium

- *Nannochloropsis limnetica* stock culture

**Steps:**

1.  Sterilise material.

    1)  Clean bench with ethanol.

    2)  Put the following material under UV light in the flow hood for 20 minutes:

        - Phytoplankton COMBO medium

        - Pipettes with tips

        - Skandi burner

        - Tubes to be attached inside the bottle.

2.  Attach tubes inside the bottle.

3.  Inoculate.

    1)  Place *Nannochloropsis limnetica* starter culture under the flow hood.

    2)  Use Skandi burner to burn around the stock culture lid.

    3)  Add 10 ml of it to 2 L of Phytoplankton COMBO medium (remember to shake).

4.  Clean bench with ethanol.

5.  Label bottle.

6.  Aerate. Set up the *Microcystis aeruginosa* culture (in Phytoplankton COMBO medium) to get oxygenated by plugging its tube into an aquarium pump using a tube connector. Remember to first get air in and then open the clump.
