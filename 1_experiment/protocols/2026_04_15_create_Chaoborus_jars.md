**Title:** Create Chaoborus jars, sample *Chaoborus* & *Daphnia*

**Date:** Wednesday 15th April 2026

**Aim:** Starting the Chaoborus cultures, feeding them, and sampling Daphnia and Chaoborus.

**Material:**

| *Prepared* | *Amount* | *Material* | *Purpose* |
|----|----|----|----|
| ☐ | 7 | Falcon tubes (50 ml) | To sample and distributing Daphnia and Chaoborus |
| ☐ |  | Eppendorf tubes | To put the Chaoborus inside |
| ☐ | 11 L | Zoo COMBO | To create new Daphnia and Chaoborus jars |
| ☐ |  | Pasteur pipette | To suck water out of samples to be frozen |
| ☐ |  | Becker (3L) | To mix the Chaoborus larvae |

**Steps:**

1.  Prepare the Daphnia.

    1.  *Create jars.* Create 7 jars with 800 ml for the new Daphnia cohort, labelled as “EG – Today’s date – Daphnia magna – Mixed diet”.

    2.  *Pipette into newly created jars.* Pipette the adults from the oldest cohort (5 days old babies) to the new Daphnia jars. If there are less than 10 adults left, take some from the Daphnia stock populations.

    3.  *Prepare the falcon tubes for feeding & sampling.*

        1.  Pipette 15 babies from each jar of the oldest cohort into a falcon tube. Do this with six falcon tubes (4 for feeding Chaoborus, 2 for quality analysis). Remove as much water as possible from the analysis falcon tubes by using a Pasteur pipette.

        2.  Pipette 20 individuals from one of the analysis tubes into one for body size analysis.

2.  Prepare the Chaoborus.

    1.  *Take off dead.* Take off the dead larvae from the stock cultures.

    2.  *Mix.* Mix all larvae inside a 3 L Becker.

    3.  *Sample* (remove as much water as possible using a Pasteur pipette).

        1.  Take 4 times 10 Chaoborus larvae and place them inside Eppendorf tubes labelled “CLi”.

        2.  Take 10 larvae and place them in a falcon tube for body size analysis.

    4.  *Create jars.* Create 4 jars with 800 ml of Zoo COMBO for Chaoborus to be fed with Daphnia.

    5.  *Pipette into newly created jars.* Add 10 Chaoborus larvae in each of the jars.

    6.  *Feed with Daphnia.* Add from the falcon tubes created at step 1.
