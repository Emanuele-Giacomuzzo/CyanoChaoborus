**Title:** phytoplankton propagation

**Date:** Wednesday 4th February 2026

**Aim:** adding COMBO medium to phytoplankton stock cultures and propagating them. From each stock culture, we are going to create two new stock cultures of also approximately 40 ml.

**Material:**

☐ Phytoplankton stock cultures\
☐ Cell flasks

☐ COMBO Medium\
☐ Laminar flow bench\
☐ Spray flask with 70% ethanol

☐ Spray flask with 10% Bleach (10% of commercial concentration)\
☐ SKANDI burner\
☐ Pipette 20-200 µL and sterile tips\
☐ Paper towels

**Steps:**

1.  Prepare the laminar flow bench.

    1)  Clean the laminar flow bench with bleach, then ethanol. 

    2)  Place the material you’ll need it for transfer on the bench:

        1.  Cell flasks (labelled with culture number & today’s date)

        2.  COMBO medium

        3.  SKANDI burner

        4.  Pipette 20-200 µL and sterile tips

    3)  Turn on the UV-light for 30 minutes.

2.  Place the old cultures on the bench.

3.  For each flask where you are going to create a new culture:

    1)  Burn around the top with the SKANDI burner for a few seconds.

    2)  Fill with approximately 20 ml COMBO medium.

4.  Add old culture to each of the new cultures.

5.  Clean the laminar flow bench with bleach, then ethanol.

6.  Place the two newest cultures in front of each other in the culture room.

7.  Dispose of waste

    1)  Carry the oldest backup cultures upstairs to the experimental lab. Plug the sink and pour the contents out. Shake the flasks as well as you can to empty them as much as possible. Add “3 seconds” of dish soap to the sink and mix lightly. Drain the sink after app. 2 hours. 

    2)  Remove the marker from the flasks with 96% ethanol.

    3)  Wash the flasks with water to make sure there are no visible remnants of the algae in them.

    4)  Load the flasks into the dishwasher and notify the lab technicians that you’ve filled the dishwasher. If the dishwasher isn’t free, please talk to the lab technicians.
