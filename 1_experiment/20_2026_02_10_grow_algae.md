**Title:** Grow *Microcystis aeruginosa* & *Synechococcus elongatus.*

**Date:** Tuesday 10th February 2026

**Aim:** Inoculate *Microcystin aeruginosa* & *Synechococcus elongatus* in Phytoplankton COMBO. The climate room we refer to throughout this protocol is room 20-1-145 and is kept at 20 degrees Celsius.

**Material:**

- Phytoplankton COMBO medium

- *Microcystis aeruginosa* & *Synechococcus elongatus* starter cultures

**Steps:**

1.  Sterilise material.

    1)  Clean bench with ethanol.

    2)  Put the following material under UV light in the flow hood for 20 minutes:

        - Phytoplankton COMBO medium

        - Pipettes with tips

        - Skandi burner

2.  Inoculate.

    1)  Place *Microcystis aeruginosa* starter culture under the flow hood.

    2)  Use Skandi burner to burn around the stock culture lid (not too close to the aluminium foil).

    3)  Add 5 ml of it to 2 L of Phytoplankton COMBO medium (remember to shake).

3.  Clean bench with ethanol.

4.  Label bottle.

5.  Aerate. Set up cultures to get oxygenated by plugging their tubes into an aquarium pump using a tube connector. <u>Remember to first get air in and then open the clump.</u>
