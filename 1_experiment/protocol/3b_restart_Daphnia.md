**Steps:**
 
1. Create 4 buckets with 7 L of Zoo COMBO
- “EG – Today’s date – Daphnia magna – Micro & Tetra”
- “EG – Today’s date – Daphnia magna – Syneco & Tetra”
- “EG – Today’s date – Daphnia magna – Nanno 1"

2. Take the oldest cohorts (fed to damselflies yesterday) and transfer their moms onto the newly created buckets. For each bucket of the oldest cohort: 
    i. Filter Daphnia moms over an extra bucket.
    ii. Put back moms onto the new bucket pouring COMBO over the filter.  
3. Top up with the missing Daphnia moms that were recorded yesterday (to achieve 15 moms/L, i.e., 105 moms/bucket).
