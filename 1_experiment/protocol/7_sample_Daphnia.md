**Title:** Sample *Daphnia*

**Date:** Wednesday 28th October 2026

**Day:** 16

**Aim:** Sample *Daphnia magna* for their quality. We assume that the dry weight of one *Daphnia magna* of 0–5 days old is about 0.01 mg, and that PUFAs and toxicity analyses requires about 1 mg of dry weight (100 babies).

**Material:**

| *Prepared* | *Amount* | *Material* | *Purpose* |
|---|---:|---|---|
| ☐ | 240 Daphnia babies/diet | Daphnia populations | To sample |
| ☐ | 30 Daphnia adults/diet | Daphnia populations | To sample |
| ☐ | 9 | Microtubes | Store *Daphnia* for quality analysis |
| ☐ | 1 | Glass pipette | To move Daphnia |
| ☐ | 1 | Pasteur pipette | Take out liquid from the Eppendorf tubes |

**Steps:**

⚠️ Take as much water out as possible from the tubes using a pasteaur pipette.

<u>*Daphnia babies*</u>

For each diet, store babies into the following tubes:

- 40 babies onto a microtube for body size analysis (`Daphnia _____ (diet name) Size 28/10/26`). ⚠️ Add 20 ml of 95% ethanol.
- 100 babies onto a microtube for PUFAs analysis (`Daphnia _____ (diet name) PUFA 28/10/26`).
- 100 babies onto a microtube for toxin analysis (`Daphnia _____ (diet name) Toxin 28/10/26`).
    
<u>*Daphnia adults*</u>

For each diet, store adults into the following tubes:

- 15 adults onto a microtube for CN analysis (`Daphnia _____ (diet name) CN 28/10/26`). 
- 15 adults onto a microtube for P analysis (`Daphnia _____ (diet name) P 28/10/26`).
