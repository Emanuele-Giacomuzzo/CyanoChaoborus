**Title:** Sample *Daphnia*

**Date:** 

**Day:** TBD

**Aim:** Sample *Daphnia magna* for their quality. We assume that the dry weight of one *Daphnia magna* of 0–5 days old is about 0.02 mg, and that each of the following analyses requires about 1 mg of dry weight:

- PUFAs
- Sterols
- CN
- P
- Toxicity

**Material:**

| *Prepared* | *Amount* | *Material* | *Purpose* |
|---|---:|---|---|
| ☐ | 2200 Daphnia/diet | Daphnia populations | To sample |
| ☐ | 60 | Eppendorf tubes | Store *Daphnia* for quality analysis |
| ☐ | 12 | Falcon tubes | Store *Daphnia* for body size analysis |
| ☐ | 1 | Glass pipette | To move Daphnia |
| ☐ | 1 | Pasteur pipette | Take out liquid from the Eppendorf tubes |

**Steps:**

*Record body size*

1. Store 40 *Daphnia magna* onto the following tubes:

- `Daph_micro_size` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_synec_size` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_nanno_size` (4 replicates)

2. Take out as much water as possible from each sample. 
3. Add 20 ml of 95% ethanol to each tube. 

*Store samples for analyses*

2. Store 100 *Daphnia* onto each of the following Eppendorf tubes, with 4 replicates per analysis. <u>Important: (i) do NOT use alcohol, and (ii) take as much water out from the samples before freezing by using a Pasteur pipette.</u>

A. Microcystis diet:

- `Daph_micro_PUFA` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_micro_sterols` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_micro_CN` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_micro_P` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_micro_toxins` (4 replicates)

B. Synechococcus diet:

- `Daph_synec_PUFA` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_synec_sterols` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_synec_CN` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_synec_P` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_synec_toxins` (4 replicates)

C. Nannochlaropsis diet:

- `Daph_nanno_PUFA` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_nanno_sterols` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_nanno_CN` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_nanno_P` (4 replicates)
- `Daph_nanno_toxins` (4 replicates)
