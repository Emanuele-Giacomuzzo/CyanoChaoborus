**Title:** Sample *Daphnia*

**Date:** 

**Day:** 

**Aim:** Sample *Daphnia magna* for their quality. We assume that the dry weight of one *Daphnia magna* of 0–5 days old is about 0.01 mg, and that PUFAs and toxicity analyses require about 1 mg of dry weight (100 babies).

**Material:**

| *Prepared* | *Amount* | *Material* | *Purpose* |
|---|---:|---|---|
| ☐ | 240 Daphnia babies/diet | Daphnia populations | To sample |
| ☐ | 60 Daphnia adults/diet | Daphnia populations | To sample |
| ☐ | 36 | 15-ml tubes | Store *Daphnia* for quality analysis |
| ☐ | 1 | Glass pipette & bulb | To move Daphnia |
| ☐ | 1 | Daphnia light table | To see Daphnia |
| ☐ | 1 | Pasteur pipette | Take out liquid from the Eppendorf tubes |

**Steps:**

⚠️ Take as much water out as possible from the tubes using a Pasteur pipette. Also, make sure not to get any algae into the tubes.

### Daphnia babies (0-5 days old)

For each diet, store babies into the following 15-ml tubes:

| Number of Daphnia | Analysis | Label |
|---:|---|---|
| 40 | Body size | `Daphnia babies _____ (diet name) Size 30/10/26` |
| 100 | PUFAs | `Daphnia babies _____ (diet name) PUFA 30/10/26` |
| 100 | Sterols | `Daphnia babies _____ (diet name) Sterol 30/10/26` |
| 100 | CN | `Daphnia babies _____ (diet name) CN 30/10/26` |
| 100 | P | `Daphnia babies _____ (diet name) P 30/10/26` |
| 100 | Toxins | `Daphnia babies _____ (diet name) Toxin 30/10/26` |

⚠️ Add 95% ethanol to the tube used for body size analysis.
