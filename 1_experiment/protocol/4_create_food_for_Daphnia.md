**Title:** Create food for the Daphnia

**Date:** 

**Day:** 

**Aim:** Create food to feed Daphnia with different diets so that they would get fed 2 mg C/L/day (2 days: 4 mg C/L) with the following diets (percentage is intended in terms of C, not in terms of volume)

- 50% *Microcystis aeruginosa* + 50% *Tetradesmsu obliqus* 
- 50% *Synechococcus elongatus* + 50% *Tetradesmsu obliqus*
- 100% *Nannochalropsis limentica*

**Material:**

| *Prepared* | *Amount* | *Material* | *Purpose* |
|----|----|----|----|
| ☐ |  | Experimental algae cultures | Algae to be fed to Daphnia |
| ☐ | 12 L | COMBO medium (autoclaved) | To replace the algae volume taken out |
| ☐ | 12 L | Zoo COMBO medium | To replace the the algae |
| ☐ | 8 | 1 L jars | To keep the harvested algae |
| ☐ | 1 | Burner | To flame-sterilise while handling cultures |

**Steps:**

1. Turn on the spectrophotometer to get it ready to measure algae absorbance. 

*Harvest the algae*

2.  Prepare the material to take out the algae.
    i.  Prepare material to be sterilised in the laminar flow hood.
        a.  Clean your hands and flow hood with ethanol.
        b.  Put a in the flow hood:
            -  Beckers
            -  COMBO
            -  Burner
    ii.  Turn on the UV light for 30 minutes.
3.  Bring up the large algae culture. <u>When disconnecting the tube, use the clamp to prevent medium from entering the filter.</u>
4.  Take out algae into the Becker. For each culture:
    i.  Burn the top of the algal culture and take off its lid.
    ii.  Harvest onto a Becker roughly the following volumes (need to do for each of the two phytoplankton bottles):
            - Microcystis aeruginosa: 0.5 L 
            - Synechococcus elongatus: 1 L
            - Tetradesmsus obliquus: 1.5 L
            - Nannochloropsis limentica: 1.5 L 
    iii.  Add back the amount of COMBO you took out.
5.  Connect the large algal culture back to the pump.



*Replace the COMBO with the Zoo-COMBO*

6.  Create algal pellets.
    i.  Add 48 ml of algae to each centrifuge tube (<u>use the auto pipette and the pipette tip with written the name of the species of algae you are working with</u>).
    ii.  Transform the inoculated algae into a pellet by spinning the Falcon tubes for 10 minutes at 28,000 revolutions per minute.
    iii.  Throw out the liquid from the algal cultures. <u>Do this as quickly as possible after the spinning to avoid resuspension.</u>
    iv.  Add 48 ml of Zoo COMBO to each of the falcon tubes. Then mix them into a Becker.

*Calculate how much you need to mix and give to the Daphnia*

7.  Measure the optical density (OD) of the algae using a spectrophotometer. <u>The cuvette is kept inside ethanol and is washed first with demi water and then again with ethanol.</u> The cuvette is cleaned with specific paper.
    i. Set the wavelength to 480 nm (GOTOWL: 480 nm)
    ii. Auto Zero using Zoo-COMBO. 
    iii. Measure the OD of each alga. 
8. Plug the OD onto the food calculator to know how to mix the algae to create different diets.

*Store food*

9. Store created food in the 4 °C room.
