**Title:** Sample Ischnura 

**Date:** 

**Day:** 

**Aim:** Sample *Ischnura* for their quality. We assume that the dry weight of one *Ischnura* is about 2 mg, and that each of the following analyses requires about 1 mg of dry weight. 

**Material:**

| *Prepared* | *Amount* | *Material* | *Purpose* |
|----|----|----|----|
| ☐ | Stock *Ischnura* | 1 | Used  |
| ☐ | Millimetre paper | 1 | Have a sense os scale in the photos |
| ☐ | Stereo microscope | 1 | Take photos of *Ischnura* |
| ☐ | Eppendorf tubes | 20 | Store damselflies for analysis |
| ☐ | Pasteaur pipette |  | Take out liquid from the Eppendorf tubes |

**Steps:**

⚠️ Take as much water out as possible from the tubes using a pasteaur pipette.

For each damselfly:

1. Wash with COMBO. 
2. Take a photo with mm paper. 
3. Freeze in a microtube with the following label: `D_____ (damselfly number) 27/10/26`
