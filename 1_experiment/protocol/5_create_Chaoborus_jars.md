**Title:** Create *Chaoborus* jars, sample *Chaoborus* & *Daphnia*

**Date:**

**Aim:** Start the *Chaoborus* cultures, feed them, and sample *Daphnia* and *Chaoborus*.

**Material:**

| *Prepared* | *Amount* | *Material* | *Purpose* |
|----|----|----|----|
| ☐ | 7 | Falcon tubes (50 mL) | Sample and distribute *Daphnia* and *Chaoborus* |
| ☐ |  | Eppendorf tubes | Store *Chaoborus* samples |
| ☐ | 11 L | Zoo COMBO | Prepare new cultures |
| ☐ |  | Pasteur pipette | Remove excess water |
| ☐ |  | 3 L beaker | Mix *Chaoborus* larvae |

**Steps:**

*Prepare the Daphnia*

1. **Create jars.** Prepare seven 800 mL jars labelled:
   > **EG – Today's date – *Daphnia magna* – Mixed diet**
2. **Transfer adults.** Move adults from the oldest cohort (5-day-old offspring). If fewer than 10 adults remain, supplement them from the stock cultures.
3. **Prepare Falcon tubes.**
   1. Place 15 juveniles from each oldest-cohort jar into six Falcon tubes (4 for feeding and 2 for quality analysis).
   2. Remove as much water as possible from the quality-analysis tubes using a Pasteur pipette.
   3. Transfer 20 individuals from one quality-analysis tube into a separate Falcon tube for body size analysis.

*Prepare the Chaoborus*

1. **Remove dead larvae.**
2. **Mix** all remaining larvae in a 3 L beaker.
3. **Collect samples.**
   1. Remove excess water with a Pasteur pipette.
   2. Place four groups of 10 larvae into Eppendorf tubes labelled **CLi**.
   3. Place 10 larvae into one Falcon tube for body size analysis.
4. **Create jars.** Prepare four 800 mL jars containing Zoo COMBO.
5. **Transfer larvae.** Add 10 *Chaoborus* larvae to each jar.
6. **Feed.** Add *Daphnia* from the Falcon tubes prepared in Step 1.
