**Title:** Sample Ischnura 

**Date:** Monday 12th October 2026

**Day:**

**Aim:** Sample *Ischnura* for their quality baseline. We assume that the dry weight of one *Ischnura* is about 2 mg, and that each of the following analyses requires about 1 mg of dry weight: 

- PUFAs
- Sterols
- N
- P
- Toxicity

**Material:**

| *Prepared* | *Amount* | *Material* | *Purpose* |
|----|----|----|----|
| ☐ | Stock *Ischnura* | 1 | Used  |
| ☐ | Millimetre paper | 1 | Have a sense os scale in the photos |
| ☐ | Stereo microscope | 1 | Take photos of *Ischnura* |
| ☐ | Eppendorf tubes | 20 | Store damselflies for analysis |
| ☐ | Pasteaur pipette |  | Take out liquid from the Eppendorf tubes |

**Steps:**

*Record body size*

1. Take photos of 10 *Ischnura* from the stock population. Use a millimetre paper inside the petri dish. 
2. Take photos of each of the *Ischnura* from the experiment. 

*Store samples for analyses*

3. Store 1 damsefly onto each of the following Eppendorf tubes, with 4 replicates per analysis (<u>Important: (i) Do NOT to use alchool, (ii) Take as much water out from the samples before freezing by using a Pasteur pipette</u>):

- Isch_day_0_PUFA (4 replicates)
- Isch_day_0_sterols (4 replicates)
- Isch_day_0_CN (4 replicates)
- Isch_day_0_P (4 replicates)
- Isch_day_0_toxins (4 replicates)

**Calculations:**

- Eppendorf tubes = 5 analyses · 4 tubes/analysis = 20 tubes
