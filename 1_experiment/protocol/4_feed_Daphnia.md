**Title:** Create food mix for Daphnia

**Date:** 

**Aim:** Feeding *Tetradesmus obliquus* and *Synechococcus elongatus* (both in Zoo-COMBO) in equal amount of carbon to Daphnia to provide them with 2 mg C/L.

**Material:**

| *Prepared* | *Amount* | *Material* | *Purpose* |
|----|----|----|----|
| ☐ |  | *Tetradesmus obliquus* and *Synechococcus elongatus* large cultures | Algae to be measured and fed to Daphnia |
| ☐ | 2 L | COMBO medium (sterilised) | To replace the algae volume taken out |
| ☐ | 2 | Beckers (≥ 1 L) | To hold the algae during preparation |
| ☐ | 1 | Burner | To flame-sterilise while handling cultures |

**Steps:**

*Take out the food*

1.  Prepare the material.
    1.  Prepare material to be sterilised in the laminar flow hood.
        1.  Clean your hands and flow hood with ethanol.
        2.  Put a in the flow hood:
            1.  2 Beckers
            2.  COMBO (to replace what we took out)
            3.  Burner
    2.  Turn on the UV light for 20 minutes.
2.  Bring up the large algae culture. <u>When disconnecting the tube, use the clamp to prevent medium from entering the filter.</u>
3.  Take out algae into the Becker. For each culture:
    1.  Burn the top of the algal culture and take off its lid.
    2.  Add 1 litre of the algal culture to the Becker
    3.  Add 1 litre of COMBO to the large algal culture*.*
4.  Connect the large algal culture back to the pump.
5.  Reclean the bench with ethanol and put away stuff.

*Replace the COMBO with the Zoo-COMBO*

6.  Create algal pellets.
    1.  Add 48 ml of algae to each centrifuge tube (<u>use the auto pipette and the pipette tip with written the name of the species of algae you are working with</u>).
    2.  Transform the inoculated algae into a pellet by spinning the Falcon tubes for 10 minutes at 28,000 revolutions per minute.
    3.  Meanwhile you are waiting for the centrifuge to be done, turn on the spectrophotometer.
    4.  Throw out the liquid (phytoplankton COMBO) from the algal cultures. <u>Do this as quickly as possible after the spinning to avoid resuspension.</u>
    5.  Add 48 ml of Zoo COMBO to each of the falcon tubes. Then mix them into a Becker.

*Calculate how much you need to mix and give to the Daphnia*

7.  Measure the optical density (OD) of the algae using a spectrophotometer (needs 15 minutes from when you turn it on to when you can use it). <u>The cuvette is kept inside ethanol and is washed first with demi water and then again with ethanol.</u> The cuvette is cleaned with specific paper.
    1.  Measure Zoo-COMBO alone by selecting photometric \> enter.
    2.  Measure the algae by selecting GOTOWL: 480 nm; start.
8.  Mix the algae so that half of carbon comes from *Tetradesmus* and half comes from *Synechococcus*.
9.  Provide with 2 mg C/L the Daphnia cultures kept on mixed diet.
