**Title:** Create Daphnia cohorts

**Date:** 

**Day:** 

**Aim:** Creating new cohorts of *Daphnia*, which will be raised for five days on 

- 30% *Microcystis aeruginosa* + 70% *Tetradesmsu obliqus*
- 30% *Synechococcus elongatus* + 70% *Tetradesmsu obliqus*
- 100% *Nannochalropsis limentica*. 

which will then be fed to *Chaoborus*.

**Material:**

| *Prepared* | *Amount* | *Material* | *Purpose* |
|----|----|----|----|
| ☐ |  | Daphnia stock populations | Source of individuals to seed the buckets |
| ☐ | 35 L | Zoo-COMBO | Culture medium |
| ☐ | 1 | Glass pipette and pipette bulb | To transfer Daphnia individuals |
| ☐ | 1 | Daphnia light table | To see individuals while pipetting |

**Steps:**
 
1.  Create 5 buckets with 7 L of Zoo COMBO (15 adult Daphnia/L)
- “EG – Today’s date – Daphnia magna – Micro 1”.
- “EG – Today’s date – Daphnia magna – Micro 2”.
- “EG – Today’s date – Daphnia magna – Synec”.
- “EG – Today’s date – Daphnia magna – Nanno 1.
- “EG – Today’s date – Daphnia magna – Nanno 2”.
2.  Pipette 105 *Daphnia magna* adults from the stock cultures into each newly created bucket. 
3. Feed with 2 mg C/L (1 mg C/L/day, as it will last them two days) of their respective diets.
