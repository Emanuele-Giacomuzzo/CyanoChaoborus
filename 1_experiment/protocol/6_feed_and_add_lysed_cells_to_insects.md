**Steps:**

*Check the damselflies and replace their medium*

1. Check which damselflies have eaten all the Daphnia, had died, or molted (write below). 
2. Make 32 new jars for the damselflies. 
3. Add 60 ml of COMBO and a straw to each new jar.
4. For each damselfly, pour it with medium onto the sieve and using forceps transfer it to its new jar. 

*Feed Ischnura with Daphnia*

1. For each diet, filter Daphnia from the oldest cohort (now babies 0-5 days old) and keep babies in petri dishes. 
2. Count how many babies the Microcystis diet produced (least productive diet), which will determine the number of babies which will be provided to each damselfliy (write below).
3. Pipette babies you want to feed onto falcon tubes. 
4. Determine how many Daphnia moms are left in each bucket (write below). The lowest amount will determine how many moms will be added/replaced tomorrow (also write below).
5. Feed the Daphnia babies to the *Ischnura*.

*Add dissolved toxins to Ischnura*

1. Unfreeze tube with *Microcystis aeruginosa* in Zoo-COMBO.
2. Sonicate the tube for 60 minutes with two cooler packs.
3. Centrigue at 28,000 rpm for 10 minutes.
4. Pour supernatant into a jar. 
5. Add _______ ml of supernatant to the jars receiving dissolved toxins (toxins produced by the amount of Microcystis matching the average density of Microcystis to which the Daphnia offered were exposed to).
