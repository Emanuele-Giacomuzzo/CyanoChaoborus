# Optical density 

You can find here all the data to reconstruct the relationship between optical density (OD) at the wavelength 480 nm. All the species are characterised in the zoo-COMBO meaning a medium composed of COMBO without nitrogen and phosphorus, to stop the growth of algae.

Where information comes from: 

- Nannnochloropsis limentica: find information and original typewritten data in the lab book CyanoChaborus Notebook 1 at page 21.
- Mycrocistis aeruginosa: find information and original typewritten data in the lab book CyanoChaborus Notebook 1 at page 50.
- Nannnochloropsis limentica + Mycrocistis aeruginosa packaging: find information and original typewritten data in the lab book CyanoChaborus Notebook 1 at page 55.

What the different files: 

- Volume added: the amount of volume added to the filtration system
- OD: optical density at 480 nm
