# Rules for AI

Guidelines for any AI assistant (e.g. Claude) writing or editing content in this repository.

1. Even though input from the team may be written in a messy, informal, or stream-of-consciousness way, everything written *into* the repository (README files, documentation, notes, etc.) should be as clean and polished as possible.
2. Use bullet lists, numbered lists, tables, etc. when they help organize information (e.g. team member lists, step-by-step protocols, comparisons). Otherwise, use well-structured paragraphs.
3. Keep formatting consistent and symmetric across similar entries (e.g. every team member listed with the same structure: name, role, institution).
